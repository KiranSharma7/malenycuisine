## Home Page (https://www.malenycuisine.com.au/)

Hand crafted on Queensland's Sunshine Coast, from locally sourced Australian fresh produce.

Inspired by the abundance of fresh fruit and vegetables in Queensland, the first Maleny Cuisine recipes were developed in 1988 when an opening in the market was discovered for gourmet condiments that were gluten free, yet delicious and affordable. Each of the recipes were created based on ripe seasonal fruit and veggies, with minimum processing required to ensure maximum freshness. As a result, each product in the Maleny Cuisine range makes a statement about being a pure gourmet condiment, authentic in flavour and appeal.

Now, over 35 years later and still running as a family owned and operated business, we remain true to the original vision for the range by continuing to produce gourmet quality premium condiments that are made from the freshest, locally sourced produce. We pride ourselves in producing these great tasting condiments to enhance the flavours of your everyday dishes or add something special to your entertaining experiences.

## FAQ (https://www.malenycuisine.com.au/faq/)

Heading: MALENY CUISINE FAQ

**Are your products vegan?**
All our bottled products are suitable for a vegan diet as we do not use any ingredients derived from animals in any these products. Christmas puddings are not suitable for a vegan diet, as they contain egg whites.

**How long do products keep once opened?**
For maximum flavour and freshness, we recommend that you consume the products within 2-3 weeks after opening. However, some products (especially the dressings) are fine in a fridge for up to 6 weeks.

**Shelf life?**
You will find a best before stamp on each jar, but most of our products have a shelf life of approximately 18 to 20 months from the date of production when unopened.

**Where can I buy?**
You can purchase your products online through our website. To find a stockist in your area please use the stockists tab where you will be able to search stockists by postcode.

**Are your products low sodium?**
Apart from the BBQ Sauce, all our other products are classified as low in sodium and are suitable for anyone following a low sodium diet. We do not even have salt in our kitchen! The BBQ Sauce has soy sauce as one of the ingredients which increases the sodium content beyond what is considered to be a low sodium product.

**Company history**
Maleny Cuisine was founded in the town of Maleny nearly 30 years ago. The production moved down to the coast in Marcoola for logistical reasons about 15 years ago, but as it was a well-established brand here in Queensland the name Maleny Cuisine was retained. We still use the same great recipes and are still very proud to be a local Sunshine Coast company.
